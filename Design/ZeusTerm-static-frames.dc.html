ZeusTerm terminal-first project scanner — concept directions

ZeusTerm scans your whole Mac and renders every detected repo as a star in a constellation — clustered by project type, colored by git status. It's a terminal at heart: click a star and the shell cd's straight into the project. Drill into one and its branches & commits become their own constellation (the work-tree map you asked for).

Below: ① the Constellation Hub (dark), ② the git work-tree map, ③ light mode, ④ one project's many worktrees in orbit. Stars twinkle live. No OS chrome, per your call.
